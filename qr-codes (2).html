Entry & Exit QR codes

One entry QR and one exit QR per lot, printed at that lot's own gates. Scanning either takes the driver straight to the right form for that specific lot — the lot is baked into the QR's URL, so there's no extra redirect step to build.

Each code is generated live by a free public QR API (api.qrserver.com) from <base URL>/entry-form.html?lot=<lot id> or .../exit-form.html?lot=<lot id>. Update the base URL once you deploy, click regenerate, and re-print.
